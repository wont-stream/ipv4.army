## Smart Home

I love having control over my stuff at the touch of a button.

I use **<Link href="https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Matter_(standard)">Matter</Link> over <Link href="https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Thread_(network_protocol)">Thread</Link>** and stick to it as much as possible. I use **Google Home** to keep things simple and intergrated with my Android ecosystem.

For lighting, **Philips Hue** is my bulb of choice — they run <Link href="https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Zigbee">Zigbee</Link>, but I'm convinced nothing beats them.
